Information We Handle

Pawstorm does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal information to play. The game stores gameplay information locally on your device, including selected companion, unlocked stages, stars, best completion times, and music, sound, and vibration preferences. This local data is used only to provide and preserve the game experience.

You may remove locally stored game data by deleting the app or using any in-app reset option that may be provided. Deleting the app may also remove unsynchronized progress.

Advertising and App Tracking Transparency

Pawstorm may display advertising after certain gameplay milestones. Where required, the app requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before initializing advertising services that may track activity across apps and websites. You can allow or deny this request; Pawstorm remains playable either way.

If advertising is available, the advertising provider may process information in accordance with its own privacy policy and your device settings. We encourage you to review the privacy information presented by Apple and any advertising provider used by the app. You can change an app's tracking permission in iOS Settings at any time.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share the locally stored game-progress data described above with third parties. Advertising providers, when enabled, may receive information necessary to deliver and measure ads under their own terms and privacy policies.

Data Storage and Security

Your game progress and settings are kept locally on your device. While we use reasonable care in designing the app, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. Please keep your device and operating system up to date.

Children's Privacy

Pawstorm is a family-friendly game and is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, please contact us so that we can address the issue.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

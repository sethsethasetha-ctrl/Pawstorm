Information We Handle

Pawstorm does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal information in order to play. The game saves selected companion, unlocked stages, stars, best completion times, and music, sound, and vibration preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to operate and preserve the game experience.

You can remove local game data by deleting the app or using an in-app reset option, if available. Deleting the app may remove progress that has not been synchronized elsewhere.

Advertising and App Tracking Transparency

Pawstorm may display advertising after certain gameplay milestones. Where required, the app requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework before advertising services that may track activity across apps and websites are initialized. You may allow or deny this request; Pawstorm remains playable either way.

If advertising is available, the advertising provider may process information under its own privacy policy and your device settings. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings at any time.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. Locally stored game progress is not shared with third parties. Advertising providers, when enabled, may receive information necessary to deliver and measure ads under their own terms and privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

Pawstorm is a family-friendly game and is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us so we can address the issue.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

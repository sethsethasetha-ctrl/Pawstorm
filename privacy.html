1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Zichen Play Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Pawstorm (the “Game”). We designed Pawstorm to work without an account and to keep core game progress on your device.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Pawstorm stores gameplay information locally using Apple-provided device storage. This may include unlocked levels, earned stars, best completion times, your selected companion, and preferences for music, sound effects, vibration, and advertising milestones. This information is used to save your progress and settings. We do not receive this local information unless a feature or service described below transmits related data.

3. Advertising and Tracking Permission

Pawstorm may display advertising at selected gameplay milestones. On supported Apple devices, the Game may ask for permission through Apple’s AppTrackingTransparency framework before any tracking activity that requires permission. You may allow or deny the request. Denying permission does not prevent you from playing Pawstorm.

When an advertising provider is enabled, it may process limited technical and advertising information such as your device type, operating system version, IP address, advertising identifier when legally permitted, approximate location derived from IP address, ad impressions, and interactions with ads. The exact data used depends on your permission choice, region, device settings, and the provider’s configuration.

4. Security and Fraud Prevention

We may use a security service to protect the Game, advertising systems, and users from fraud or abuse. A security provider may process technical signals such as app version, device model, operating system version, network information, IP address, diagnostic events, and device or app integrity signals. These signals are used for security and fraud prevention rather than to provide a public user profile.

5. Information We Do Not Request Directly

Pawstorm does not require you to create an account. The Game does not directly ask for your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health information, or payment card details. Any App Store purchase and billing information is handled by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

6. Purposes of Processing

  • Save game progress and preferences on your device.
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve game performance.
  • Deliver and measure advertising where enabled.
  • Protect Pawstorm and its users against fraud, abuse, and security threats.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited information may be processed by service providers acting on our behalf, including advertising, security, hosting, and technical service providers. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

8. Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you reset or delete it, uninstall the Game, or the operating system removes it. Service providers may retain technical, security, or advertising information according to their own retention schedules and applicable law. We seek to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You can change music, sound, and vibration preferences inside the Game. You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings and may limit personalized advertising through your Apple device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection regarding personal information. Contact us to make a request.

10. Children’s Privacy

Pawstorm uses child-friendly visuals and contains no violent or graphic content. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and tracking features must be configured to follow applicable age, consent, platform, and regional requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and delete it where required.

11. International Processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers and require providers to handle information in accordance with applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Pawstorm, our service providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date above and provide any additional notice required by law.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Zichen Play Studio at support@zichenplaystudio.com.
